ALL IN ONE.

DYNK replaces your card terminal and your ad spend with a single platform — lower fees, instant settlement, and a direct line to the customers already near you.

1¢
Flat Transaction Fee

Every card processor takes 2–3% of every sale. DYNK settles on-chain for a flat 1¢ — a coffee or a car, always the same one cent. No percentage, no middleman, no surprises.

400ms
Instant Settlement

Funds arrive in your non-custodial wallet in 400ms. DYNK never holds your money — your keys, your funds, always. No waiting 2–3 business days for what you already earned.

Market Into Their Wallet

DYNK is the only platform that puts your offer directly inside nearby customers' wallets. Set a deal, pick your audience, and your ad lands where they're already making decisions — not a feed, not an inbox.

One Dashboard, Everything

Accept payments, run campaigns, and track your revenue in real time — all from a single dashboard built for the floor, not the back office. Revenue auto-splits into tax, wages, and ops buckets before you think about it.
